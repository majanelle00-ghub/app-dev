# My Favorite Series: *Stranger Things*

**Why I like it:**
- Great story and characters.
- 80s nostalgia and suspense.
- The mystery and supernatural elements keep me hooked every season.
- Friendship and family themes are heartwarming.

**Main characters**
1. Eleven — a mysterious girl with telekinetic powers who escaped from a lab.
2. Mike — leader of the friend group who befriends Eleven.
3. Dustin — funny and smart, always loyal to his friends.
4. Lucas — brave and logical member of the group.
5. Will — the boy who goes missing in Season 1.

**My favorite season:** Season 1 — it perfectly introduces the Upside Down and the main characters.

**Favorite quote:**
> “Friends don’t lie.” — Eleven

**Recommended for:** viewers who like sci-fi, mystery, and emotional stories about friendship.
